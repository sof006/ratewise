Switch Bluetooth Headphones to High quality (LDAC).

Steps
  1. Run: pw-cli set-param bluez_card.00_00_00_00_00_01 Profile '{ index: 131080, save: true }'
     Switches Bluetooth Headphones to High quality (LDAC) (profile a2dp-sink).

Notes
  - Sound on Bluetooth Headphones stops for a moment while it switches.

Afterwards
  WirePlumber remembers this profile for the device, including after it reconnects.

`ratewise revert` undoes this.
